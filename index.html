My Sandy, ♡

I have been thinking about what you told me. I understand now that you were not asking me to talk to you every second or to come up with something exciting to say. You just wanted me to think of you and text you on my own.

When I went quiet for hours and came back later like nothing happened, I can see how that made you feel forgotten. And when you tried to explain it, I kept saying sorry instead of really listening. You should not have had to explain the same feeling again and again just to be understood.

I am sorry for making you feel like you had to remind me to include you in my day. A little good morning, a random thought, something funny I saw, or telling you about my day might seem small, but I understand that those little things help you feel close to me.

I am still learning, but I do not want that to become an excuse. I cannot promise to be on my phone every minute, but I can make a real effort to reach out first, share the little things with you, and tell you when I am busy instead of leaving you wondering.

You do not have to forget that you were hurt, and you do not have to forgive me straight away. I just want you to know that I heard you. I love being part of your everyday life, and I want you to feel that you are part of mine too.

Thank you for being honest with me, even when it was frustrating to explain. I want to be someone who listens and follows through, not someone who only says the right things when you are upset.

I want to love you better,
Maniee ♡
